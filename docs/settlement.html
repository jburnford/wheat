Settling the prairies, 1879–1935

The Dominion Land Survey checkerboard: CPR land sales (odd sections, shaded by price/acre) interlock with homestead entries (even sections, shaded by settlement year), with the railway network growing year by year. Zoom out for township totals; zoom in for quarter sections. Scrub the years to watch the prairie fill in.

Data: CPR land sales — University of Calgary Archives · Homestead records — David Allan, Jim Clifford & Cheryl Troupe, HGIS Lab, University of Saskatchewan · Railways — University of Toronto. Quarter-section geometry is an open reconstruction of the public DLS survey grid.
