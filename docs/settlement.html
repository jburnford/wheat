Settling Saskatchewan, 1879–1935 WORK IN PROGRESS

The Dominion Land Survey checkerboard: CPR land sales (shaded by price/acre) interlock with Crown→private land transitions from the homestead registers (shaded by tenure type — homestead, sale, scrip, railway grant, …) and surrendered First Nations reserve land, with the railway network and incorporated towns appearing year by year. Zoom in for individual quarter sections; scrub the years to watch the province fill in.

⚠ Work in progress. The land-register layer is still being transcribed — most quarter sections aren't entered yet, and many lack a tenure type or date; the Métis communities layer is also being added to. Treat gaps as "not yet transcribed," not "nothing there." (The CPR, reserve and town layers are complete.)

Data (all work in progress): CPR land sales — University of Calgary Archives · Homestead records — David Allen, Jim Clifford & Cheryl Troupe · Reserves & Métis communities — Ashley Rabbitskin, Julian Rioux, Jim Clifford & Cheryl Troupe (HGIS Lab, University of Saskatchewan) · Towns & cities (1921) — Jessica Jack, part of the Mapping Settler Colonialism in Saskatchewan project (Clifford & Troupe), University of Saskatchewan · Railways — University of Toronto. Register data refreshed 25 Aug 2026 (58% of quarter sections transcribed).
