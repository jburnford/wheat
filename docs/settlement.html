Settling Saskatchewan, 1879–1935 WORK IN PROGRESS

The Dominion Land Survey checkerboard: CPR land sales (odd sections, shaded by price/acre) interlock with homestead entries (even sections, shaded by settlement year) and surrendered First Nations reserve land, with the railway network growing year by year. Zoom in for individual quarter sections; scrub the years to watch the province fill in.

⚠ Work in progress. Much data entry remains — homestead, reserve and Métis coverage is incomplete and being added to. Treat gaps as "not yet transcribed," not "nothing there."

Data (all work in progress): CPR land sales — University of Calgary Archives · Homestead records — David Allan, Jim Clifford & Cheryl Troupe · Reserves & Métis communities — Ashley Rabbitskin, Julian Rioux, Jim Clifford & Cheryl Troupe (HGIS Lab, University of Saskatchewan) · Railways — University of Toronto.
